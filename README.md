## 👨‍💻 The Elevator Pitch

I am a computer science student specializing in **Machine Learning and Data Science**, bridging the gap between theoretical algorithms and practical, deployable solutions. I build end-to-end ML pipelines—from data ingestion and preprocessing to model deployment and interactive visualization. 

My current focus is on **Explainable AI (XAI)**, predictive analytics, and optimizing computer vision models for real-time edge performance. 

* **🌱 Currently Learning:** Advanced GenAI workflows and MLOps (Docker, Kubernetes).
* **🤝 Open To:** Roles in Data Science / ML Engineering, Open-source collaborations, and research initiatives.
* **⚡ Fun Fact:** I enjoy breaking down complex mathematical ML concepts into intuitive, easy-to-understand explanations.
